# W2 handoff

## Done
- Early-warning screen with 2025 date selection, timeline, play/pause, A010 preset and visible historical replay banner.
- Cards use receipt-aware three-day active warning window, grouped per asset, with vibration/temperature triggers and two SVG trends. Valid received readings only; missing intervals stay gaps.
- Readings source drawers resolve actual asset/date IDs; retrospective evidence links to readings and matched events.
- Sample inventory availability, alternative sites, reorder/transfer selection and linked request confirmation. A request never increases stock.
- Session identity, role and site checks in warning decisions. Acknowledgement and staging retries are idempotent. False alarms require a reason and remain in Reviewed.
- Raised-warning activity logs once per alert across replay and remounts. No render/update feedback loop.
- Summary widget exports asset and sample-stock shortage counts.

## How to see it
- Sign in as the demo planner and open #/early-warning.
- Initial view defaults to 2025-01-07 and A010. Stage the missing sample pump bearing part by rush reorder or select an alternative-site transfer. Follow the parts queue link.
- Acknowledge or flag another warning; return across replay dates to see retained decisions.
- Technician/operations-manager sessions see read-only controls; site lead can stage within assigned scope.
