## Known gaps
- Guide core O4 $668k is the sum of already-rounded site figures. Exact event recomputation is $667,402.03 and 731.718 hours, for $93,000 budget; the model retains this source precision. Full all-site O4 gives $842,354.15 and 967.992 hours.
- Preset comparison costs exclude HSE by default, as in Findings 2.2. The optional HSE switch adds historically open action costs and hours once; this does not authorize a pilot.
- The stock-fill-to-O4 sensitivity and adoption scaling are explicit illustrative linear assumptions, not fitted causal relationships or stock quantities inferred from spare fill. Source base benefits are reached at 90% assumed fill and 100% adoption.
- The model charges a full annual cost and starts benefit after the later of specified deployment or maximum option lead time. Named D1 estimates stay fixed and labeled rather than overwriting custom model calculations.
